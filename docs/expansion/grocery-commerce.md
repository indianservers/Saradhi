# Grocery commerce

Grocery can use the shared category, product, variant, price, stock, cart, parent-order and merchant-child-order model. Checkout rejects fractional quantities for `each` units, and rejects variable-weight products until a final-weigh and payment-authorization adjustment flow exists. Inventory has reservations and kitchen/pick-pack task foundations.

Batch/expiry tracking, barcode workflows, substitution preference/approval, missing-item handling, partial fulfillment and controlled variable-weight final billing are not implemented. No silent charge increase is supported.
