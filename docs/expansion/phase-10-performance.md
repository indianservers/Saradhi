# Phase 10 performance evidence

No 10,000-order/day capacity claim is made. The Phase 2.B/2.C local smoke evidence is functional, narrow, and not representative of multiple cities, large catalogues, checkout contention, POS sync or recurring workloads. Phase 10 adds no representative load test or production benchmark. Required measurements include database/lock latency, scheduler lag, connection-pool use, geospatial query latency, cache hit rate, CPU/memory and error rate under a workload profile.
