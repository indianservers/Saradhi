## Visual observations

- All four boards use Quickbuddy wordmarks and a light, premium operating style. Customer branding adds orange and violet; admin uses a blue-violet accent with orange highlights.
- Mobile boards present five portrait screens across each row with numbered captions. The merchant board clips the lower portion of its second row at the source image edge; those screens must be completed from their visible design language, and this limitation is not evidence that the target contains no lower controls.
- Admin panels use a persistent narrow left navigation, compact filter/search toolbar, small metric cards, dense tables, status chips and charts. The City Operations screen is the map-heavy exception.
- Product screens use photo-led cards. Customer surfaces favor orange purchase actions, violet recommendations and chips, rounded cards and a persistent bottom navigation. Pilot and restaurant surfaces use larger operational controls and higher information density.
- The original bitmap boards are large but the embedded screen content is small. Crops in `docs/ui/reference-crops/` preserve each screen's source pixels for implementation comparisons; they are reference material only, not production app photography.

## Reference imagery audit

No separate product image library or font files were found in the application folders at audit. The boards show realistic food photography but do not identify its license or source. Do not extract the board photos into production. Use licensed local assets or commissioned/generated imagery with a recorded source before claiming photography parity.
