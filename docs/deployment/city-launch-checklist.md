# Controlled city/zone launch checklist

1. Approve city, zone, category and named operational owner.
2. Verify emergency pause flags pause new intake without hiding or deleting existing obligations.
3. Complete legal/privacy, support escalation, merchant KYC and pilot onboarding.
4. Confirm inventory, operating hours, fees, tax, delivery zones and capacity.
5. Test order, merchant acceptance, dispatch, GPS freshness, delivery completion, refund/COD and reconciliation on isolated data.
6. Validate contact paths, incident alerts, worker/Redis recovery, DB backup/restore, dashboard access and rollback.
7. Begin with internal test zone and small approved cohort; record thresholds and stop conditions.
8. Expand only after daily financial and operational reconciliation and written decision.

Pause ordering by city/zone/category via the approved feature control. Continue tracking outstanding orders, refunds, COD custody and customer communication; drain or cancel according to status policy. Do not enable broad COD by default. No city is approved by this checklist alone.
