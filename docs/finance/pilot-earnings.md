# Pilot earnings

Typed pilot payable and settlement records exist, but current dispatch completion does not calculate a final versioned pilot earning payable. Estimates shown by AI/logistics must not be treated as posted earnings. COD cash received, pilot compensation, tips, incentives, and adjustments are separate financial concepts.

Before payout use, define city/category compensation schedules, distance/time sources, incentives with idempotent source event identities, adjustments and approvals, payable posting, statements, and payout reconciliation. Do not net cash custody against pilot earnings without an approved accounting policy.
