# Privacy and consent

Customer-owned `GET/POST /api/v1/me/consents` records append-only marketing choices by channel with a policy version, server-recorded source/time and idempotency. Opting into email requires verified email; SMS/WhatsApp requires verified phone. Admin Customer 360 shows the latest consent events separately from notification preferences. Consent does not automatically enroll customers in campaigns; no campaign sender is enabled.

The ledger does not offer access/correction/deletion request processing, retention policies, anonymization or customer-specific export approval. Flutter UI controls have not been added. Exact address text and coordinates are withheld from CRM views.
